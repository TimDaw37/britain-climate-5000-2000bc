# Citations

All series plotted on this site were downloaded from public palaeoclimate archives on 12 September 2026.

1. Charman, D.J., Blundell, A., Chiverrell, R.C., Hendon, D. and Langdon, P.G. (2006).    Data: SISAL v3 / NOAA https://doi.org/10.25921/zpm6-m889
